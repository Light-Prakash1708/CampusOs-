# Student guide

## Signing up

- **Your college uses CampusOS:** use the invitation link from your college, or sign up and choose **Join your college**. Enter your department, programme, year and roll number, and the college office approves you.
- **Your college doesn't use it yet:** create a free personal workspace (18 or older). When your college joins, request to join. Your tracker, progress and saved items move with you.
  - Can't find your college? Tell us in "College not listed?". When enough students from one college ask, we contact them. We never share your name.
- **Under 18:** join through your college's invitation. Personal sign-up is for adults only.

## What you get

- **Attendance.** Your standing in each course against the college minimum, and how many classes you can still miss (or need to attend).
- **Notices.** Every official notice in one place. Some ask you to tap **I've read this**, so the office knows you saw them.
- **Redressal.** Raise a grievance. You see the deadline the college must meet and can appeal to the Ombudsperson if you're not satisfied. You can file anonymously where the category allows it.
- **Growth (free).** A tracker, skills and career goals, and opportunities.
- **Install it.** On Android, use "Add to Home screen". Offline, it shows a simple offline page. It never stores your personal data on the phone.

## Your privacy

- The privacy notice (`/privacy`) says what is collected and why.
- CampusOS does not rank you or give you a risk score. When staff see a signal about you, such as "below minimum in DBMS", it states the reason.
- Account → Privacy (`/account/privacy`) lets you **download your data**. In a personal workspace you can also **delete it**.
- Your college's data belongs to your college. Ask the college office about deleting college records.
